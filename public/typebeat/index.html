How it works

  • Letters play drums. Each letter is one tiny step in time.
  • Space or . is a rest: a quiet step.
  • ( ) squeeze the letters inside into triplets (three notes where two would fit).
  • CAPITALS play loud. lowercase plays medium. That is dynamics!
  • Try typing your name. Whose name has the coolest rhythm?
