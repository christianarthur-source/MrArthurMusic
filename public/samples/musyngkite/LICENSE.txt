MusyngKite instrument samples
=============================

The MP3 files in this folder are a subset of the "MusyngKite" General MIDI soundfont,
as published by Benjamin Gleitzman in the project gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts:

  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
  https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/MusyngKite/

Only the instruments and notes used by Mr. Arthur's Staff Composer are included
(33 instruments, one sample every three semitones from A0 to C8). The files are
byte-for-byte copies of the originals, downloaded on 2026-11-16, not modified.
